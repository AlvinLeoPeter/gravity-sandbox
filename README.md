# AI Gesture-Controlled Gravity Simulator

Physics mini project (SRM Ramapuram, 2026). Throw planets around a star with hand gestures in front of your webcam, and compare the Euler, Euler–Cromer and RK4 numerical methods.

**Open it:** https://alvinleopeter.github.io/gravity-sandbox/ (Google Chrome, allow camera access)

- Pinch thumb and index finger to grab, move to aim, open your fingers to launch.
- No webcam? Use the mouse: click, drag and release.
- Hand tracking runs fully in the browser (MediaPipe Hands); no video leaves the computer.
